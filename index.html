About Me

Hello! I’m James Westhead, a 2025 BEng Software Engineering graduate (2:1) from Sheffield Hallam University. I have hands-on experience across the full stack, working with technologies such as Python, React.js, C#, and more.

My projects range from automating folder organization, transferring music playlists, to developing robotics systems and intelligent software tools. Through these, I have developed a passion for writing maintainable, modular code and building solutions that scale in the real world.

I focus on creating software that is user-friendly and practical, ensuring that every project not only works well technically but also delivers a great experience for the end-user.
